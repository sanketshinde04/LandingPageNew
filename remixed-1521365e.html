// FAQ

Asked, answered.

I'm not a strong coder. Can I keep up? +
Yes — if you can read basic Python, you're in. The modern agent stack does the heavy lifting; we teach you to direct it. Plenty of past attendees were PMs and consultants, and they shipped.
What if I don't have a "real problem" to bring? +
You do — you just haven't framed it yet. In Week 1 we run a problem-scoping session that turns "my inbox is chaos" or "research takes me 6 hours" into a buildable agent spec.
How is this different from the 8-week GenAI Launchpad? +
This is its sharper successor. We cut survey-style breadth, went all-in on agents, and replaced the single capstone with two Ship Weeks — so it's shorter, denser, and you build twice as much.
What if I miss a live session? +
Every session is recorded and posted same-day. Ship Weeks are async-friendly by design — office hours run daily, and your 1:1 review is scheduled around you.
Will my company pay for this? +
Usually, yes — most seats are employer-sponsored L&D. We provide an invoice and a manager-ready justification doc. Team packs of 3+ get a discount.
Which tools and models do we use? +
The current production stack: Claude & GPT model families, MCP, LangGraph, CrewAI, vector stores, LangSmith-style tracing, plus Claude Code for agentic development. The stack refreshes every cohort.
